Get involved

Join the NeuroQSP Working Group.

Membership is free and open to anyone working at the intersection of pharmacology, modeling, and neuroscience. Sign up to receive seminar invitations, join discussions, and help shape shared workflows.

What membership gives you

Be part of the conversation.

  • Seminar invitations — monthly talks from leaders across QSP and neuroscience.
  • Community access — connect with peers across academia, industry, and regulators.
  • Shared resources — workflows, reading, models, and tools as they grow.
  • A chance to contribute — present your work or help lead an initiative.

Prefer email?

Write to us directly at neuroqsp@gmail.com and we'll add you to the list.
