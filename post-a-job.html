    Posting guidelines

    What we accept — and what keeps the board useful.

    A few rules so that every listing is worth a reader's time.

    ✅ What belongs here

    Positions relevant to quantitative systems pharmacology, pharmacometrics / PKPD, computational neuroscience, translational CNS biomarkers, or CNS drug discovery & development — at any level, in any sector.

    🚫 What we won't publish

    Unrelated roles, recruiter aggregations or scraped listings, multi-level marketing, unpaid "opportunities" pitched as jobs, duplicates, and anything without a working application link or email.

    🔗 A real way to apply

    Every listing must include a direct https:// application link and/or a contact email, plus any instructions. We check that it works before approving.

    ⏳ Nothing goes stale

    Listings expire after 60 days by default, or on your stated deadline (90 days max). To keep a role listed longer, reopen your submission with a new deadline. When it's filled, close it and it disappears within a day.

    🛡️ How we keep spam out

    Nothing appears without human approval. Submissions pass an automated verification check and rate limit, and include a contact email so moderators can verify the poster. Abusive sources are blocked.

    ✏️ Edits & removals

    To change or remove a listing, email neuroqsp@gmail.com and quote your reference number. Removals are usually done within a day.
