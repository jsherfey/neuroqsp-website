NeuroQSP Working Group

Quantitative systems pharmacology, built for the brain.

We unite mechanistic modeling, pharmacology, and human neuroscience to improve decision-making across CNS drug discovery and development — from molecular target to clinical outcome.

Why we exist

Translation is the central challenge in CNS R&D.

Brain drug programs fail more often, and later, than in almost any other therapeutic area. The gap between a molecular mechanism and a clinical effect is wide — spanning exposure, target engagement, circuit dynamics, and profound patient heterogeneity.

NeuroQSP builds the shared language and practical workflows that connect these scales, so quantitative models can actually inform the decisions that matter: which dose, which biomarker, which patient, which trial.

What we do

Bridging models across every scale of the CNS.

We connect the modeling traditions that rarely talk to each other — and the data that should inform them.

PK / PD & QSP

PBPK, PKPD, and systems-biology models that quantify exposure, target engagement, and downstream pharmacology.

Computational neuroscience

Biophysical circuits, electrophysiology, and network models that turn mechanism into measurable neural dynamics.

Human neuroscience data

EEG/MEG, imaging, and behavior linked back to model parameters as translational, clinically anchored readouts.

Where it pays off

Model-informed decisions across the pipeline.

The point of building shared workflows is to make better calls, earlier. NeuroQSP methods support decisions that are otherwise made on intuition alone:

  • Dose selection grounded in exposure and target engagement.
  • Biomarker strategy that connects molecular action to neural readouts.
  • Patient stratification that accounts for biological heterogeneity.
  • Trial design informed by quantitative, mechanistic prediction.
