How it works

Three steps, no account on our site.

1

Fill in the form

Title, organization, location, a short description, and how to apply. Takes about five minutes. You'll sign in with a free GitHub account, which keeps submissions accountable.

2

We review it

A NeuroQSP moderator checks that it's a genuine, relevant position — usually within a few days. You'll get a notification when it's approved.

3

It goes live — and retires itself

Your listing appears on the job board with a clear apply link. It's removed automatically after 60 days (or your deadline), so the board never shows stale roles.

Posting guidelines

What we accept — and what keeps the board useful.

A few rules so that every listing is worth a reader's time.

✅ What belongs here

Positions relevant to quantitative systems pharmacology, pharmacometrics / PKPD, computational neuroscience, translational CNS biomarkers, or CNS drug discovery & development — at any level, in any sector.

🚫 What we won't publish

Unrelated roles, recruiter aggregations or scraped listings, multi-level marketing, unpaid "opportunities" pitched as jobs, duplicates, and anything without a working application link or email.

🔗 A real way to apply

Every listing must include a direct https:// application link and/or a contact email, plus any instructions. We check that it works before approving.

⏳ Nothing goes stale

Listings expire after 60 days by default, or on your stated deadline (90 days max). To keep a role listed longer, reopen your submission with a new deadline. When it's filled, close it and it disappears within a day.

🛡️ How we keep spam out

Nothing appears without human approval. Submissions require a signed-in account and a moderator-only contact email so we can verify the poster. Abusive accounts are blocked.

✏️ Edits & removals

Edit your submission any time — changes are re-reviewed and republished automatically. Need a listing taken down fast? Close the submission or email neuroqsp@gmail.com.
