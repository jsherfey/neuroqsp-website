About the working group

Connecting mechanism to clinical outcome in the brain.

NeuroQSP is a community of modelers, pharmacologists, and neuroscientists working to make quantitative, mechanistic modeling a standard part of how CNS therapies are discovered and developed.

Our mission

A shared language for quantitative neuropharmacology.

We combine mechanistic modeling, pharmacology, and human neuroscience to improve decision-making in brain drug discovery — translating molecular mechanisms into clinical outcomes.

Quantitative systems pharmacology (QSP) has transformed how many therapeutic areas reason about dose, mechanism, and response. The brain has lagged — its models are fragmented across disciplines that use different tools, vocabularies, and data. NeuroQSP exists to close that gap and build workflows practitioners can actually use.

The scope of our work

Three modeling worlds, one continuum.

Each captures part of the path from drug to effect. NeuroQSP connects them.

Exposure & pharmacology

PBPK, PKPD, and systems-biology models that describe how a molecule reaches the brain, engages its target, and perturbs downstream pathways.

Circuits & dynamics

Computational neuroscience — biophysical neurons, networks, and electrophysiology — translating molecular perturbations into neural activity.

Human readouts

EEG/MEG, imaging, and behavior that anchor models to measurable, clinically meaningful signals in real patients.

What we're building toward

Our goals.

  • Establish shared workflows and vocabulary across QSP and neuroscience.
  • Account for exposure, target engagement, and patient heterogeneity in a single framework.
  • Link molecular mechanisms to clinical-level outcomes with quantitative rigor.
  • Support real decisions: dose selection, biomarkers, stratification, and trial design.
  • Grow an open, cross-sector community across academia, industry, and regulators.
